#### 🙋 **Classroom Challenge Game: ISO 25010 Product Quality Characteristics Match-Up (10 Questions)**

Question 1: [ATM Cash Dispenser Failure] A customer withdraws $1,000 from an ATM. The system deducts the amount from the account balance and prints a transaction receipt, but the cash dispenser mechanism jams and dispenses no bills while the account remains debited. Which ISO 25010 product quality characteristic is primarily violated?

A) Functional Suitability  
B) Reliability  
C) Performance Efficiency  
D) Usability  
E) Security  
F) Maintainability  
G) Portability  
H) Compatibility  
Time: 20

<details>
<summary>Click to view Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer**: A
**Explanation**:
* **Option A is correct**: **Functional Suitability** represents the degree to which a product provides functions that meet stated and implied needs, specifically Functional Correctness (providing correct results with the needed degree of precision). Deducting balance without dispensing the cash is a failure of the core business function.
* **Option B note**: While triggered by a hardware fault, the fundamental issue is that the transactional business capability failed to produce the correct functional outcome.
</details>

---

Question 2: [Midnight Flash Sale Traffic Spike] During an e-commerce midnight flash sale, 500,000 shoppers arrive simultaneously. Server CPU utilization surges to 100%, API response latency escalates from 150ms to 40 seconds, and mass connection timeouts occur. Which ISO 25010 quality characteristic is primarily being tested or degraded?

A) Functional Suitability  
B) Reliability  
C) Performance Efficiency  
D) Usability  
E) Security  
F) Maintainability  
G) Portability  
H) Compatibility  
Time: 20

<details>
<summary>Click to view Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer**: C
**Explanation**:
* **Option C is correct**: **Performance Efficiency** evaluates performance relative to the amount of resources used under stated conditions, including Time Behavior (response times, throughput) and Capacity (handling maximum peak loads). Severe latency surges and CPU saturation are prototypical performance efficiency concerns.
</details>

A) Functional Suitability  
B) Reliability  
C) Performance Efficiency  
D) Usability  
E) Security  
F) Maintainability  
G) Portability  
H) Compatibility

**Explanation**:

Which ISO 25010 quality characteristic is severely deficient?

A) Functional Suitability  
B) Reliability  
C) Performance Efficiency  
D) Usability

H) Compatibility

**Explanation**:

</details>

Which ISO 25010 quality characteristic does this critical vulnerability violate?
