

## Humans and Agents in Software Engineering Loops

[https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/humans-and-agents.html](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/humans-and-agents.html)


**Summary:**
This article explores how software developers should adapt their working models as AI becomes increasingly capable of generating code. The author argues against both micromanaging AI-generated code line-by-line ("humans in the loop") and blindly trusting the AI to produce results with no oversight ("humans outside the loop"). Instead, developers should operate "on the loop" via "Harness Engineering"—building, testing, and improving the automated checks, specifications, and workflows that guide the AI agents. Ultimately, this approach leads to an "agentic flywheel" where AI can analyze its own workflows and recommend improvements to the harness itself, resulting in continuously evolving systems.


## The Future of Software Engineering: Retreat Findings and Strategic Insights

[https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/report/tw_future%20_of_software_development_retreat_%20key_takeaways.pdf](https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/report/tw_future%20_of_software_development_retreat_%20key_takeaways.pdf)

這份報告總結了 Thoughtworks 於 2026 年舉辦的技術論壇精華，探討 人工智慧如何徹底重塑軟體工程 的未來面貌。內容指出開發者的核心工作正從直接撰寫程式碼，轉向監督與引導 AI 代理人的**「中迴圈」工作模式**。論壇強調工程嚴謹性將轉移至規格審查、測試驅動開發以及風險控管，而非傳統的人工代碼審閱。此外，企業必須面對 AI 代理人架構帶來的組織挑戰，包括決策瓶頸與安全隱患的增加。報告同時反思了開發者職能的轉型，指出初級工程師與資深架構師在 AI 協作環境下的角色演變與挑戰。最後，作者提醒企業需建立全新的技術底層與治理框架，以應對系統複雜度超越人類認知能力的風險。
